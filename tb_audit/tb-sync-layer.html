Mechanism: every case created on the pharmacist device is written to a local queue immediately and is fully usable offline — nothing blocks on connectivity. A background sync trigger checks connectivity opportunistically; when a connection is available, each queued case is sent to the central verifier queue and marked synced, with a retry-on-failure log if a transfer is interrupted.
